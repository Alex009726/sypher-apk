Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy explains what information Sypher processes, why it is used, which service providers help operate the app, and how to make a privacy or account-deletion request.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Sypher, we collect and process:

  • Account and sign-in information: email address, name or display name, username, account identifier, and (for email/password accounts) a password hash and verification records. If you sign in with Google, we receive the Google account identifier and the verified email address and name supplied in the sign-in response. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Profile and rewards information: optional profile details, wallet address you provide, referral relationships, mining sessions and balances, streaks, task participation, ad-view and reward records, and policy-acceptance records.
  • Device and security information: IP address, device, browser and app information, and a device fingerprint used to operate the service, secure accounts, enforce limits, and detect duplicate or abusive activity. The app may also receive push-notification tokens if you enable notifications.
  • Face-verification information: if you request face verification, the app captures and uploads a selfie. Sypher’s backend temporarily handles the image and sends it to Didit for liveness checks and duplicate-face search. Didit returns verification and face-match results; Sypher stores the provider’s request reference and the verification outcome. Sypher’s backend does not save the uploaded image as a permanent file. Didit’s handling and retention of the image and biometric/face data are also subject to Didit’s own policies.
  • Advertising information: when ads are available, Google AdMob may process ad interactions and device/ad identifiers, along with information such as IP address and app/device details, under Google’s own privacy terms.

2. How we use information

We use this information to create and secure accounts; provide sign-in, mining, rewards, referrals, tasks, and support; process wallet and profile settings; deliver account and security emails; verify real people and prevent duplicate or fraudulent activity; serve and verify ads; maintain the service; and meet legal or security obligations.

3. Service providers

Sypher uses service providers to operate the app:

  • Google provides Google sign-in and, through AdMob, advertising services.
  • Didit processes selfies for liveness and duplicate-face verification.
  • Resend delivers verification codes, password-recovery, and account-related emails. It receives the recipient address and email delivery information.
  • Render hosts Sypher’s backend API.
  • Supabase hosts the PostgreSQL database used for Sypher account and service records. Sypher’s backend connects to that database; the app does not directly connect to Supabase.

These providers process information as needed to provide their services and under their own applicable privacy terms. Sypher does not sell personal information. We may also disclose information when legally required or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or our rights.

4. Retention and account deletion

We keep information while it is needed to provide the service, protect accounts, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. To request account deletion, email chibuzormarvellous739@gmail.com from your account email address and identify the Sypher account. We will verify the request. When deletion is accepted, account access is disabled and the account is scheduled for permanent deletion after a 30-day grace period. At purge, Sypher also requests deletion of associated Didit verification records where supported. Some information may be retained longer when necessary for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution.

The current Profile screen’s deletion UI is not connected to the deletion API. Please use the email process above to request deletion.

5. Your choices and privacy requests

You may ask us to provide, correct, or delete your personal information, subject to applicable law and necessary verification. Contact us using the email above. You can also manage app permissions, including camera and notifications, through your device settings. Google and Didit may provide additional choices through their own services.

6. Security and international processing

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Our providers may process information in locations outside your own; their terms and safeguards apply to that processing.

7. Children

Sypher should only be used by people who meet the minimum age required by applicable law in their location. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page. For privacy questions or requests, contact chibuzormarvellous739@gmail.com.
